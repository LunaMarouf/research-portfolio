What it was

The Ontario Engineering Society runs an annual multi-discipline student competition across Ontario engineering schools. As biomedical competition lead I designed the competition package for the biomedical event, the problem statement, rules and judging structure that teams would compete against.

The competition did not run that year due to scheduling issues on the host side, so there is no event result to show. The design work itself was completed.
