Started as a research and development member across several subteams, then became electrical team lead, managing the electrical subteam through engine harness integration and sensor work.

Lassonde Motorsports, Formula SAE Club · May 2025 to October 2025

01

What It Was

Formula SAE is a student design competition in which teams build a small formula-style race car and are judged on engineering design as well as track performance. It runs across mechanical, electrical and vehicle-dynamics subteams that all have to converge on one car.

I joined as a research and development member, producing short technical writeups that different subteams used to make design decisions, then moved into leading the electrical subteam of nine members.

02

Research and Development Member

Short technical research feeding subteam design decisions

  • Researched differential options across subteams and made the case for a Torsen differential, which the team adopted
  • Produced short technical documents on a range of subteam topics as decisions came up

Additional R&D writeups to be added as documents are collected.

03

Electrical Team Lead

Managing a nine-member subteam through the harness and sensor build

  • Led and managed the nine-member electrical subteam
  • Engine wiring harness integration
  • ECU input and output mapping
  • Sensor selection and integration work
  • Review of the shutdown and interlock circuits
