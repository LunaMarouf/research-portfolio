Monaco Energy Boat Challenge, mechanical and electrical design.

Altium PCB schematics for the propulsion power circuits, boat CAD, and FEA simulation to minimise weight and von Mises stress while maximising safety factor, on an international student energy-boat entry.

CAN-YU, Monaco Energy Boat Challenge · Mechanical and Electrical Team Member · June 2025 to July 2026

01

What it was

The Monaco Energy Boat Challenge is an international competition for student-built boats using alternative propulsion. Entries are judged on energy efficiency and engineering design, which puts weight on both a hull that is structurally sound but not overbuilt, and electronics that instrument the vessel well enough to know where the energy is going.

I worked across both sides of the CAN-YU entry, research and schematic capture for the propulsion electronics, and CAD and structural simulation on the boat itself.

02

Electrical design

  • Research supporting propulsion and power-system decisions
  • Altium Designer schematic capture for the propulsion system's power circuits
  • Multi-sensor PCB schematics covering propulsion, sensing and control
03

Mechanical design

  • CAD modelling of the boat
  • FEA simulation of the hull and structural components
  • Design iteration to minimise weight and von Mises stress while maximising safety factor
