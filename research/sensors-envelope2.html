See the cytochrome c platform Email
01

Why a Second Device

The straight-channel cytochrome c sensor proved the imprinting and fabrication route end to end, but its electrode geometry was never optimised, it grew out of what was practical to lay out first. Envelope 2 is a structural glycoprotein on the hepatitis C virus surface, and a working sensor for it is a more clinically relevant target than cytochrome c, which is a general liver-damage marker rather than an HCV-specific one.

Rather than reuse the straight-channel mask, the circular device was designed from the electrode geometry up. A 3 mm circular working electrode gives uniform current distribution across its area, and reduced edge current density 22% versus the baseline geometry, promoting uniform mass transport and film growth. The counter electrode is oversized so current is never limited there, and the reference is placed close in to minimise iR drop. It is built on the same batch spray-fabrication process as the first device, not a hand-made one-off, with standard operating procedures covering electrode fabrication, surface functionalisation, electropolymerisation and bench testing to keep the test sequence consistent across prototype batches.

02

Device Architecture and Fabrication

Designed rather than improvised

Masks laser cut on an Epilog Fusion Pro 17000 at 65% speed and 15% power on medium-tack PSA, then sprayed in panels. Graphene through a 0.5 mm nozzle, Ag/AgCl through 0.8 mm, both at 4 bar and 23 L/min.

Full layer-stack detail and spray parameters follow the same process as the straight-channel device, see the cytochrome c fabrication section. Quality screening for this device is covered below.

Baseline Electrochemical Characterisation

Characterising the cell before running an analyte on it

Triplicate data per technique across several bare devices, 5 mM ferri/ferrocyanide in 0.1 M KCl unless noted. These characterise the empty cell, not an Envelope 2 response. No Envelope 2 concentration response has been measured yet. Full test conditions and code are the same manifest-driven pipeline used on the cytochrome c platform, see the software section.

0.35 V was the only trial potential where the current settled to a plateau rather than climbing indefinitely, which is why it was carried forward as the electropolymerisation setpoint elsewhere on this page. Trial potentials above 0.35 V also show sharply rising initial-current variance across devices (SD nearly matching the mean at 0.45 V), a second reason to prefer the lower potential.

04

Quality Assurance, Examples of Rejected Devices

Deciding which chips are worth testing

Electrochemical testing is slow and reagents are finite, so every device is inspected under the microscope first. Three rejection triggers are applied, one example of each shown below. Ag/AgCl contamination on the working electrode, reference-electrode breakage or non-uniformity, and conductive contact between the working and reference electrodes. An accepted device is shown in section 05.

Screening is an aid, not a validated defect classifier. Devices it keeps still go through dry isolation and continuity checks, open circuit potential stability, and full electrochemical validation before any result is trusted.

Current Status and What's Next

This is the live part of the platform

Done so far

  • Circular electrode geometry designed and fabricated in batch panels
  • Baseline EIS, DPV and linear sweep characterisation across multiple devices
  • Monomer oxidation onset confirmed against a blank, ready for PmPD film growth
  • Quality-screening applied using the same microscopy pipeline as the straight-channel device, see sections 04 and 05 above

In progress

  • Growing and characterising the Envelope 2 imprinted film on the circular geometry
  • Establishing a concentration response for Envelope 2, the same way the cytochrome c calibration curve was built
  • Comparing signal-off behaviour and variance against the straight-channel platform to see whether the geometry change actually helps
  • Second manuscript in preparation, covering this device

This page will be updated as results come in. Nothing here reports an Envelope 2 concentration response yet, that is the open question this device exists to answer.
