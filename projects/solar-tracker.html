Floating dual-axis solar tracker.

A pontoon-mounted tracker that keeps a panel pointed at the sun on water, with the drive train verified against AGMA gear criteria.

Design project · System integration and verification

01

What it was

A dual-axis tracker rotates a solar panel in two directions so it stays pointed at the sun through the day, which raises yield over a fixed panel. Mounting one on pontoons adds the problem of doing this on a platform that is itself moving.

I worked on system integration and verification, and on the mechanical analysis of the drive. Concept selection used a weighted decision matrix, and the rack-and-pinion drive was checked against AGMA pitting and bending criteria with the calculations automated in MATLAB.

02

What I did

  • System integration and verification.
  • Concept selection by weighted decision matrix.
  • AGMA gear train calculations for the rack and pinion drive, automated in MATLAB.
  • Arduino-based control with light-sensing feedback.
