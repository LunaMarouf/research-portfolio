Electrospun membranes for air filtration and reverse osmosis coatings.

Producing polymer membranes by electrospinning, and using a designed experiment to understand which process parameters actually control the result.

Freire-Gormaly Lab, York University · Researcher · April 2024 to August 2025

01

What it was

Electrospinning draws a polymer solution into fine fibres using a high electric field, building up a non-woven mat whose fibre diameter and areal density depend on solution properties and process settings. The resulting membranes are of interest both for air filtration and as coatings for reverse osmosis.

I worked on polyvinyl alcohol and chitosan membranes, and on the experimental design used to relate spinning parameters to the membranes produced. A multilevel factorial design in MATLAB was used rather than varying one factor at a time, so interactions between parameters were visible.

02

What I did

  • Electrospun PVA and chitosan membranes.
  • Multilevel factorial design of experiments, implemented in MATLAB.
  • Air filtration and reverse osmosis coating applications.
