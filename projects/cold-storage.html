Cold Storage Facility Design for Ontario Berries.

A Thermodynamics 2 course project designing a −18°C cold storage facility for individually quick frozen Ontario berries, built in CoolSelector.

Thermodynamics 2, York University · Course project

01

The Brief

Design a cold storage facility for preserving locally grown Ontario berries, blueberries, raspberries, strawberries, gooseberries and red currants, after they have been harvested, cleaned and individually quick frozen. The facility had to maintain a storage temperature of −18°C and a storage life of 30 to 365 days, holding the specified temperature and humidity conditions while accounting for energy efficiency and operational parameters.

The design work was carried out in CoolSelector, refrigeration system selection and sizing software, rather than as a CAD exercise.

02

What I Did

  • Refrigeration system design and component selection in CoolSelector
  • Sized the system to hold −18°C storage temperature across a 30 to 365 day storage life
  • Accounted for humidity control and energy efficiency in the operating parameters

R452A ran at a higher COP under these conditions, 1.11 against 0.83, but at substantially higher evaporating and condensing pressures, which pushes back on compressor and component sizing. GWP came out higher for R452A in this comparison (2140 against 1430), so the efficiency gain here is a system-design trade-off against R134a, not a straightforwardly better refrigerant on every axis.
