Monaco Energy Boat Challenge, sensing and control electronics.

Multi-sensor PCB schematics in Altium for propulsion, sensing and control, on an international student energy-boat entry.

CAN-YU · Mechanical and Electrical Design Member · June 2025 to July 2026

01

What it was

The Monaco Energy Boat Challenge is an international competition for student-built boats using alternative propulsion. Entries are judged on energy efficiency and engineering design, which puts weight on instrumenting the vessel well enough to know where the energy is going.

I worked on the electronics side of the CAN-YU entry, producing the schematic capture for the boards that handle propulsion control and the sensing needed to monitor it.
