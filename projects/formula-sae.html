Formula SAE, electrical team lead.

Led the electrical team for York University's Formula SAE entry, covering the engine harness, the ECU interface and the safety circuits.

Lassonde Motorsports, Formula SAE Club · Electrical Team Lead · May 2025 to October 2025

01

What it was

Formula SAE is a student design competition in which teams build a small formula-style race car and are judged on engineering design as well as track performance. The electrical subsystem covers everything from the engine wiring harness to the driver-facing controls and the mandated safety circuits.

I led the electrical team of nine. The role was part design and part integration, since the electrical system has to meet the competition's technical rules while fitting around mechanical packaging decisions being made in parallel.

02

What I did

  • Led a nine-member electrical team.
  • Engine harness integration.
  • ECU input and output mapping.
  • Review of the shutdown and interlock circuits.
