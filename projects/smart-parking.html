Smart parking guidance with gas sensing.

A parking lot system that guides drivers to vacant spots instead of leaving them to search, with an MQ-2 and VOC sensor pair that switches on a ventilation fan once vehicle-fume levels cross a set threshold.

Course project · LabVIEW and Arduino

01

What it was

The core problem was directing drivers straight to a vacant parking spot rather than having them circle the lot looking for one. A second system layered on top monitors air quality: an MQ-2 gas sensor and a VOC sensor watch for the kind of fume levels a running engine produces, tested in development with a lighter as a stand-in fume source, and once readings cross a threshold a small fan switches on.

The vacancy-guidance logic and the gas-sensing and fan control were both built with a combination of LabVIEW for the control and monitoring side and Arduino for the sensor and actuator hardware.

02

What I did

  • Designed the vacant-spot guidance logic
  • Integrated an MQ-2 gas sensor and a VOC sensor for fume detection
  • Threshold-triggered fan control to ventilate on elevated fume readings
  • Built the control and monitoring system in LabVIEW with Arduino hardware
